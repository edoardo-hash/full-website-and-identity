About this villa

Perched above the turquoise waters of Cala Vadella, Villa Capella is a private hillside retreat offering panoramic sea views, partial sunset vistas, and walking-distance access to the beach.

Elevated to capture the beauty of Ibiza’s southwest coastline, the villa combines privacy, generous outdoor living, and comfortable accommodation in one of the island’s most desirable residential areas.

Accessed through a private courtyard, the property opens onto expansive terraces and landscaped gardens that frame the Mediterranean. The centrepiece of the exterior is a large swimming pool surrounded by multiple lounge areas, creating an inviting setting from morning through late afternoon as the light softens over the bay.

An outdoor kitchen and bar area allow for al fresco dining and entertaining, while various terraces offer different viewpoints to enjoy the sea and partial sunset colours. Inside the main house, seven double bedrooms are thoughtfully distributed to ensure comfort and privacy for guests. Five bathrooms serve the property, providing practicality for larger groups.

The interiors are bright and welcoming, with neutral tones and abundant natural light enhancing the sense of space. A fully equipped kitchen connects smoothly to both indoor and outdoor dining areas, supporting relaxed family meals or larger gatherings.

The combination of elevated sea views, partial sunset outlook, landscaped gardens, generous terraces, and proximity to the beach makes Villa Capella particularly appealing for those seeking a coastal residence with both privacy and easy access to the shoreline.
