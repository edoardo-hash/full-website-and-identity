# Client offer pages — four licensed villas (Invenio Homes)

The private selection for 4–11 July 2027: `/` and `/villas/<villa>/` for Can Savasana, Can Octavia, Can Cecilia and
Can Vista Cala Jondal, ordered by price. Static pages, no build step. Made on 31 Oct 2026 from the five-villa build at
invenio-offer.vercel.app, with "Design Critique: Invenio offer pages (five villas)" applied.

- **Split by brand.** The original mixed Villa Capella (Non Touristic) with four licensed villas. Licensed villas go out
  as Invenio Homes (this folder); Villa Capella is its own Lila Villas offer (`../offer`). The agent sends both links.
- **No licence words for clients.** "Licensed" removed from the cards; the licence number stays on every villa.
  No Non Touristic disclaimer: all four are licensed.
- **The advisor is the contact.** No Invenio email, phone, WhatsApp number or portal link; replies go to the advisor
  (WhatsApp with the villa, dates and price written, or an email with no fixed recipient).
- Features grouped (Outdoors, Indoors, Views, Good for, Good to know); internal tags dropped (Filming, Private,
  Events, Weddings — events depend on the owner's answer for each booking).
- Taglines in one style; copy edits on the raw listing texts (Can Savasana, Can Octavia).
- Price as visible as the villa name; small text at 4.5:1 or better, nothing under 11px, 44px tap areas on phones.
- Maps: the area link (Google Maps search of the area) is kept; no exact GPS was in the source, so no ~1 km circle.
