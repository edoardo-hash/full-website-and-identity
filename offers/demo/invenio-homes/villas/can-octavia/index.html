About this villa

Can Octavia is a six-bedroom traditional finca which has been lovingly restored and transformed into a captivating home, retaining all of its authenticity and charm, yet refurbished to ensure that your every luxury and comfort is catered for. Situated in the heart of the lush green countryside of San Lorenzo, the location offers easy access to the beautiful beaches of the North, including Benirras, Cala Xarraca and Portinatx.

Ibiza Town, with its restaurants and nightlife, is a short drive away.

Each room within Can Octavia exudes local style and charm, with whitewashed walls and traditional features such as stone mason walls being perfectly complemented by Bohemian chic style furnishings and decorations. The interior design is exceptional with careful attention to detail throughout. The welcoming living room is complete with an open fireplace and flooded with natural light, and is served by a stylish and well appointed kitchen.

There are several places to relax and reflect within this charming home.

The 6 bedrooms are all decorated as the living areas, with beautiful furnishings throughout and you will find your every need catered for in all of the sleeping and bathroom areas.

Main house – 3 bedrooms, 4 bathroom, Living room, Dinning room, Bar, Kitchen

Side house – 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, Living room, Dinning room, Kitchen
