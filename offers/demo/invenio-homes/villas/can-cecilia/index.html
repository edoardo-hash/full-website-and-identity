About this villa

Set among the quiet orchards and terracotta tracks of San Lorenzo, this newly built six-bedroom villa channels the unhurried soul of rural Ibiza with rare architectural conviction. Thick whitewashed walls, timber-beamed ceilings and hand-selected fixtures speak to the island's vernacular tradition, while the generous scale of the interiors — flowing living rooms, a sophisticated library gallery, a dedicated dining room, a wine cellar and a fully equipped Siemens kitchen — offers every comfort a large family or group of friends could ask for.

Santa Eulalia's restaurants and the wild northern beaches are both within easy reach, yet once inside these walls, the outside world recedes gracefully. Picture an unhurried morning: coffee in the sun-warmed courtyard, the hills of San Lorenzo perfectly still, nowhere you need to be.
