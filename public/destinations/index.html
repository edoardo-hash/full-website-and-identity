Four destinations, one standard

Ibiza, Formentera, Mykonos and Bali — different islands, the same way of working: vetted houses, local people and a portfolio you can trust.

Explore the portal

The original · Ibiza

Ibiza, known from the inside

Ibiza is where Invenio Homes began. We know the island's villages, coves and back roads — and the houses that sit on them. The portfolio runs from the pine woods of the north to the sunset coast of the west and the hills above Cala Jondal.

Every property is licensed, vetted and looked after by people who live here. For agents, that means accurate availability and a fast answer; for owners, it means a team that knows the house as well as they do.

Search the portfolio

Twenty minutes south · Formentera

Formentera, at a slower pace

Twenty minutes by boat from Ibiza, Formentera is white sand, shallow water and a rhythm that is entirely its own. The island is small, but the houses are some of the most restful in the Mediterranean.

Our Formentera portfolio is supported by local partners and the same account team. Tell us the dates and the group; we will tell you which house works.

Search the portfolio

The Aegean · Mykonos

Mykonos, in the same hand

Cycladic light, private pools and the Aegean on three sides. Mykonos is a different sea from Ibiza, but the standard is the same: vetted houses, clear availability and a team that answers.

We work with owners and local partners across the island, from the hills above the town to the beaches of the south coast.

Search the portfolio

The long way round · Bali

Bali, for the long way round

A longer journey, a different rhythm. Bali is tropical houses, open pavilions and gardens that run into the rice fields — a natural extension of the way we think about villa living.

The portfolio is smaller and more personal, supported by local partners and the same account team. Ask us for the houses we would send our own clients to.
