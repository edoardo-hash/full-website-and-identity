S'Estanyol · Ibiza

Villa Alyssa

Stone and white walls above Ibiza Town, with sea, town and countryside views and a long pool.

S'EstanyolIbiza, Balearic Islands
6 bedrooms 6 bathrooms sleeps 12 Licence no. ETV-2115-E

Villa Alyssa stands in wide grounds just above Ibiza Town and the Marina Botafoch, close to the restaurants and the life of the port, with views over the sea to Formentera, over Dalt Vila and across the countryside.

Stone and white walls, traditional touches and a contemporary interior: generous lounges, an open-plan kitchen with a breakfast bar that opens onto the terrace, and bedrooms decorated to the same standard. The two master suites have private terraces with sea views.

Outside there is a long freshwater pool with outdoor bathrooms, a tennis court, a padel court and a small football pitch, day beds, an outdoor kitchen and bar and several places to eat outdoors. Inside, a gym, a cinema room and a pool table. The house is fully staffed, with daily cleaning, a chef and a waiter, and security at night.
