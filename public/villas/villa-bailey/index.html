Villa Bailey

A front-line cliffside villa with an infinity pool, a private path to the sea and its own boat jetty.

Es CubellsIbiza, Balearic Islands
6 bedrooms 6 bathrooms sleeps 8 Licence no. ET-0642-E

Villa Bailey is built into the cliffs at Es Cubells, on the quiet south-west coast, with uninterrupted views across the Mediterranean and the coastline. A private path leads down to a secluded beach and the villa's own boat jetty.

The house has been fully renovated, with atmospheric, modern interiors, a family kitchen with a central island and a separate professional kitchen for private chefs. Six en-suite bedrooms sleep eight guests.

The infinity pool and the carved cliff terrace are the heart of the house, with sun beds, shaded lounges and places to eat outdoors. There is a home cinema and an outdoor cinema, a games room, a squash court, a hilltop gym and wellness area, a pizza area and bar and a rooftop pavilion. Daily cleaning, a private chef and serving staff are included.
