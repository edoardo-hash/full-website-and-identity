Can Nemo

An estate in acres of gardens at Cap Martinet, five minutes from Ibiza Town, with the sea and Formentera on the horizon.

Cap MartinetIbiza, Balearic Islands
6 bedrooms 5 bathrooms sleeps 12 Licence no. ETV-1461-E

Can Nemo sits in landscaped grounds in the quiet enclave of Cap Martinet, a few hundred metres from the sea and minutes from Ibiza Town and the marinas. The house is contemporary and private, with wide terraces looking south across the Mediterranean to Formentera.

The bedrooms open onto light-filled living spaces and terraces, and the master suites have their own terraces and dressing areas. At the centre of the estate is a full wellness area — sauna, hammam, cold plunge, massage room, yoga and Pilates space and a gym — with a private padel court in the gardens.

Indoor and outdoor dining spaces, bars and a professional kitchen are made for a private chef. A team of five looks after the house, the gardens and the guests during the stay.
