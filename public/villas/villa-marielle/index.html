Villa Marielle

A private estate between San José and Cala Jondal, with a long infinity pool, a padel court and wide gardens of palms and pines.

San JoséIbiza, Balearic Islands
5 bedrooms 5 bathrooms sleeps 10 Licence no. ETV-2077-E

Villa Marielle sits in landscaped gardens of mature palms and pines between the village of San José and the bay of Cala Jondal, home to some of Ibiza's best-known beach clubs. The setting is calm and private, and well connected to the rest of the island.

Contemporary architecture meets a natural Ibiza look: polished wood floors, clean modern interiors and several lounges, dining spaces and quiet corners. Every bedroom has its own modern bathroom. The master suite is a retreat of its own, with a fireplace, a hammam, indoor and outdoor showers and a secluded Jacuzzi and chill-out area.

Outdoor living is the heart of the house: a long infinity pool, a private padel court, a pétanque pitch, an outdoor cinema and a bar and lounge made for entertaining. Wide terraces with an al fresco dining area look over a beach-style lounge.
