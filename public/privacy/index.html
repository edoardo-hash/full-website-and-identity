Privacy & cookies

We collect as little as possible, use it only to answer you and manage the relationship, and never sell it. This page explains what that means in practice.

Last updated 1 October 2026

Who we are

Invenio Homes SL, Ibiza, Balearic Islands, Spain (Balearic real-estate agents register GOIBE766172/2024), is the data controller for the information described on this page. You can reach us at find@inveniohomes.com or on +34 671 349 592.

This website introduces our portfolio and our platform. The portal is a separate application with its own terms and privacy notice.

What we collect

When you send a message, request a partner call or ask about a villa, we receive the details you choose to send: your name, email address, telephone number, company and anything else in your message.

If you become an owner, agent or partner, we will also need the details required to prepare the agreement and comply with Spanish law — identification, address and payment information, handled by our providers.

Our server records standard technical information (anonymised IP address, requested page, browser type, time) to keep the site secure and understand which pages are used. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell data.

Why we use it

We use your information to answer your enquiry, provide access to the portfolio or platform, manage a relationship with an owner or agent, meet our legal and tax obligations, and — only if you have asked — send occasional news.

The legal bases

We rely on your consent when you contact us or subscribe to our news; on the performance of a contract when you work with us; on our legitimate interest in running and securing the website; and on legal obligation for tax and accounting records.

Who sees it

Your details are seen by the people who need them to answer you or manage the relationship: our team, the owners or agents involved, and the providers who host the site, the portal and our email.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. Where a provider is outside the European Economic Area, we use a contract that meets European data protection standards.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become a relationship are kept for up to two years. Contract and invoice records are kept for as long as Spanish tax and accounting law requires. Marketing consent can be withdrawn at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, or receive it in a portable format. Write to find@inveniohomes.com and we will answer within one month.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Cookies

This site uses no advertising or analytics cookies by default. The portal uses the cookies needed to sign in and keep your session secure; those are described in the portal's own notice.

If we add analytics in future, this page will say so and you will be asked before anything is set.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with the date below. Last updated October 2026.
